Endless Tower Defence is an innovative game in TW genre.
Enemies, tower upgrades and levels never ends.
Each level has a unique path thanks to the a* algorithm.
Made with Unity, C#

# **GAME AND CONTROLS**

Every Turn has a **Random Path**

Waves become progressively stronger 

You can upgrade your towers infinitely by holding down the tower

When you kill an enemy you gain certain amount of gold

## Archer Tower 

Fast  
Deals 1 damage  
Price = 10 gold  

## Catapult Tower 

Slow  
Deals 1 damage but AoE (Area of Effect)  
Price = 15 gold  

**Upgrading towers cost 10 gold**

## Enemy Info
Mummy enemy is faster and deals 2 damage but has low hp

Beaver enemy is slow and deals 1 damage but has high hp
